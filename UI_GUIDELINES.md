# UI Guidelines

## Design Philosophy

Modern

Minimal

Clean

Professional

Accessible

Responsive

---

## Colors

Primary

Blue

Secondary

Gray

Success

Green

Warning

Amber

Danger

Red

Avoid excessive colors.

---

## Typography

Clear hierarchy.

Large headings.

Readable body text.

Consistent spacing.

---

## Layout

Mobile-first.

Maximum content width.

Consistent margins.

Generous whitespace.

---

## Components

Buttons

Cards

Inputs

Modals

Dropdowns

Tabs

Badges

Avatars

Should all follow one consistent design language.

---

## Animations

Use subtle transitions.

Avoid excessive animations.

Prefer smooth hover effects.

---

## Accessibility

Keyboard navigation.

Proper contrast.

ARIA labels where needed.

Visible focus states.

---

## Responsiveness

Support

Mobile

Tablet

Desktop

No horizontal scrolling.
